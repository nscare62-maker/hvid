Identity protection
Verification documents are reviewed once and permanently deleted. No raw identity data is stored or displayed. One human = one identity. Identity is persistent but revocable — history is always retained internally.
KYC step guide
1. Register and enter your personal details.

2. Choose and upload a government ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence.

3. Complete selfie verification and let OCR + AI review the document.

4. Your application is approved or rejected, and access is granted only after a successful review.
