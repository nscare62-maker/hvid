Core Principles

Built on verified certainties

HVID provides objective identity proof without judgment or surveillance.

Privacy First

No personal identification information is ever displayed publicly. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

One Human, One Identity

Each individual may hold a single HVID. Automated biometric and KYC duplicate checks prevent sybil accounts.

One Asset, One Owner

No digital account, handle, or website can be linked to more than one verified HVID simultaneously.

Continuous Proof

Identity continuity is verified continuously. Compromised credentials trigger immediate protective holds.

Cryptographic Audit

Public checks query cryptographic proofs. Verification status is mathematically verifiable anytime.

Minimal Claims

HVID only confirms identity status facts without behavioral scoring, algorithms, or biased labels.
