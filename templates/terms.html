SECTION 01

What HVID Is

HVID (Human Verification Identity) is an objective identity confirmation protocol and infrastructure. HVID allows individuals, enterprises, and online communities to confirm that a digital handle, communication address, or profile is linked to a registered, persistent human identity.

HVID is not a safety rating agency, social credit system, scam detection platform, or behavioral review clearinghouse. HVID does not evaluate personal morality, trustworthiness, creditworthiness, or private intentions.

SECTION 02

What HVID Provides

HVID provides the following objective, informational signals only:

  • Cryptographic confirmation that an account is linked to a verified human identity
  • Assurance level grading (Unverified, Basic, Strong, High Assurance)
  • Confirmation of identity continuity and account ownership consistency
  • System-level enforcement status (Stable, Review, Revalidation, Suspended)
Notice: These signals are strictly informational. They do not constitute an endorsement, insurance coverage, or recommendation to transact or enter into contractual relationships.

What HVID Does Not Guarantee

HVID explicitly disclaims any and all warranties or guarantees concerning:

  • The moral character, safety, integrity, or intentions of any verified user
  • The commercial or personal outcome of any transaction or interaction
  • Protection from financial loss, default, fraud, or damage resulting from dealings with any account
  • Completeness or legal validity of off-platform claims made by users

Users must exercise independent judgment and appropriate caution at all times. HVID serves as one data point among your broader diligence.

SECTION 04

User Responsibilities & Conduct

By accessing or utilizing HVID, you legally covenant and agree to:

  • Provide truthful and accurate information during enrollment and verification
  • Refrain from submitting fraudulent, weaponized, or fabricated misconduct reports
  • Never use HVID signals to harass, dox, defame, or publicly smear any individual
  • Never attempt to de-anonymize, extract, reverse-engineer, or scrape identity cryptographic hashes
  • Acknowledge that protocol outputs are informational and non-judicial determinations
