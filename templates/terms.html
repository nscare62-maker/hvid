1. What HVID Is

HVID (Human Verification Identity) is an identity confirmation infrastructure. HVID allows individuals and organisations to confirm that a digital account or identifier is linked to a registered, persistent human identity.

HVID is not a safety rating system, scam detection platform, or behavioural review service. HVID does not evaluate the honesty, intentions, or past behaviour of any person.

2. What HVID Provides

HVID provides the following informational signals only:

  • Confirmation that an account is linked to a registered human identity
  • Confirmation of identity verification level (Unverified, Basic, Strong, High Assurance)
  • Confirmation of identity continuity and ownership consistency
  • System-level enforcement status (Stable, Review, Revalidation, Limited, Suspended)

These signals are informational. They do not constitute a recommendation to engage with, transact with, or trust any person.

3. What HVID Does Not Guarantee

HVID makes no guarantee of:

  • The honesty, safety, or intentions of any verified identity
  • The outcome of any transaction, interaction, or relationship with a verified identity
  • The completeness or accuracy of any linked account information
  • Protection from financial loss, fraud, or harm arising from interactions with verified or unverified identities

Users must perform their own due diligence and exercise independent judgment in all interactions. HVID is one informational signal. It is not a substitute for personal judgment.
