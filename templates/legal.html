SECTION 01

What Data We Collect

HVID is engineered on a strict principle of data minimization. We collect only what is technically required to maintain an unforgeable identity anchor:

  • Authentication Credentials: Telephone number or email address required for multi-factor login sessions.
  • Verification Telemetry: Ephemeral selfie and document images utilized solely during active review, then permanently purged.
  • Claimed Public Assets: Social handles, web domains, and storefront URLs you explicitly request to link to your HVID.
  • Audit & Security Logs: Match query timestamps and authorization attempts for rate-limiting and anti-abuse defense.
Explicit Non-Collection: HVID never collects financial records, credit scores, real-time GPS locations, or web browsing histories outside of direct platform interactions.
SECTION 02

Encryption & Storage Architecture

All data handled by HVID is protected by institutional-grade cryptography:

  • In Transit: TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HSTS policies across all web and API endpoints.
  • At Rest: AES-256 encrypted database volumes and salted cryptographic hashing.
  • Biometric Privacy: Raw biometric captures are translated into mathematical vectors and irreversibly salted. The original image is deleted upon completed review.
SECTION 03

How Data Is Utilized

Collected information is exclusively utilized for:

  • Preventing account takeovers and automated sybil bot networks
  • Maintaining deterministic identity continuity signals across linked assets
  • Operating the private, evidence-based misconduct review queue
  • Ensuring uninterrupted protocol availability and API reliability
SECTION 04

Match Query Logging & Anti-Scraping

When an external user or platform submits a public lookup to the HVID match engine, metadata is transiently logged to detect bulk scrapers and distributed denial-of-service attempts. Query logs are never sold, never used for retargeting, and never attached to individual browsing profiles.

SECTION 05

What HVID Explicitly Never Does

  • We never sell, rent, or lease personal information to commercial third parties.
  • We never supply consumer profile data to data brokers or advertising exchanges.
  • We never perform behavioral tracking across external websites.
  • We never disclose reporter identities to reported counterparties in misconduct reviews.
SECTION 06

Data Retention and Immediate Deletion

Unprocessed verification media is automatically queued for irreversible hardware shredding within hours of review completion. Inactive accounts may request complete deletion at any time through their dashboard settings.

SECTION 07

Third-Party & Law Enforcement Disclosures

HVID does not share private data with outside parties except when legally compelled by valid court orders or statutory subpoenas under verified legal process.

SECTION 08

Statutory Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your physical jurisdiction, HVID extends full data sovereignty rights: you retain the right to access, inspect, export, correct, and irreversibly delete your account record.
