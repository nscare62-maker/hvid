Platform Integrity & Community Standards

Acceptable Use Policy

Version 2.4 · Effective: 1 April 2025 · Last updated: 12 April 2025

SAFEGUARD
Zero Public Smears

HVID outputs must never be used to harass, dox, or coordinate targeted online harassment.

ACCURACY
Evidence-Based Submissions

Fabricated, retaliatory, or speculative reports result in immediate permanent banning.

SECURITY
Anti-Scraping & Anti-Sybil

Automated scraping, multi-accounting, and bot spoofing are strictly prohibited.

SECTION 01

Permitted Uses

The HVID platform and its signals are designed solely for legitimate identity verification and trust assurance:

  • Verifying that an online counterparty, contractor, or seller is anchored to a real, accountable human.
  • Registering and linking digital handles, websites, and business domains that you lawfully own and control.
  • Submitting factual, documented misconduct claims regarding impersonation or account takeovers.
  • Querying public status signals via official user interfaces or verified enterprise API integrations.
Authorized Operation: You are encouraged to display your verified HVID badge on social channels, professional resumes, and web storefronts.
SECTION 02

Prohibited Conduct: Harassment & Public Shaming

You must NEVER utilize HVID to:

  • Intimidate, harass, threaten, or stalk any individual based on their identity status signals.
  • Publish public cancellation or accusation lists citing HVID match engine results.
  • Compile dossiers or scrape signal outputs to fuel online brigading campaigns.
  • Weaponize unverified disputes in public forums or social media.
SECTION 03

Prohibited Conduct: Privacy & De-Anonymization

You must NEVER attempt to:

  • De-anonymize or reveal private real-world identities of users beyond what they publicly declare.
  • Reverse-engineer salted cryptographic hashes stored in the identity registry.
  • Correlate HVID telemetry with third-party tracking databases or surveillance toolkits.
  • Bypass rate limits, query throttling, or privacy guards built into the public match engine.
SECTION 04

Prohibited Conduct: Fraudulent & Weaponized Reports

HVID operates an audited misconduct resolution protocol. Submitting false claims undermines the integrity of the entire ecosystem:

  • Fabricated documents, manipulated screenshots, or forged police records are strictly prohibited.
  • Filing coordinated mass reports against commercial competitors or personal rivals is grounds for immediate termination.
  • Submitting claims without factual basis, documentation, or verifiable proof.
Zero Tolerance: Intentionally filing a fraudulent misconduct claim results in permanent identity revocation and referral to judicial authorities.
SECTION 05

Prohibited Conduct: System Abuse & Scraping

You must NEVER engage in:

  • Bulk scraping, headless browser crawling, or automated harvesting of match signals.
  • Multi-accounting or sybil spoofing to circumvent suspensions on prior accounts.
  • Exploitation of application flaws, security bugs, or denial-of-service vulnerabilities.
  • Unauthorized resale, subleasing, or redistribution of HVID API key credentials.
SECTION 06

Enforcement Consequences & Remediation

Violations of this policy are actively investigated by the HVID Trust Team and subject to graduated enforcement:

  • Formal warning and temporary freeze of match query privileges.
  • Identity status downgrade to Under Review or Restricted.
  • Permanent protocol suspension and non-reversible revocation of HVID verification.
  • Formal civil litigation or criminal referral in instances of egregious fraud.
SECTION 07

Reporting Policy Violations

To report platform abuse, automated scraping, or coordinated harassment campaigns, contact our security desk with documented logs:
