Trust & Safety

What HVID does —
and what it does not.

HVID is an identity confirmation infrastructure. It confirms that a digital account is linked to a real, persistent human identity. It does not evaluate that person's honesty, intentions, or trustworthiness.

What HVID Confirms

Identity signals only

When HVID shows a verified match, it confirms the following — nothing more.

✓
Real human existence

This account is linked to a human-verified identity record. It is not an automated account or bot identity.

✓
Ownership confirmation

The person who controls this account has claimed and verified ownership of it through HVID.

✓
Identity continuity

This identity is persistent. It cannot be transferred, duplicated, or reset. History is retained.

✓
System-level status

HVID shows whether an identity is stable, under review, or subject to an enforcement action.

What HVID Does Not Guarantee

No behavioral judgment

A verified HVID does not mean the person is safe, honest, or trustworthy.

✕
Safety

HVID does not assess whether interacting with this person is safe. Always exercise independent judgment.

✕
Honesty or intent

Verification does not confirm that a person is honest, acting in good faith, or free of harmful intent.

✕
Signals, not verdicts

HVID is not a risk detection system. A verified identity does not mean a transaction is risk-free. Always exercise independent judgment.

✕
Liability for outcomes

HVID is not liable for any transaction, relationship, or interaction that occurs between users.

HVID confirms identity linkage signals.
It does not evaluate behavior, intent, or trustworthiness. All outputs are informational only and do not constitute a guarantee of any kind.

Reporting System

Private. Evidence-based. Reviewed internally.

HVID operates a private misconduct reporting system. It is not a public accusation platform.

Reports are private

Your report identity is never disclosed to the reported party. All submissions remain confidential.

Evidence-based only

Reports must contain factual evidence. Speculation, opinion, and accusations without evidence are not accepted.

Internal review

All reports are reviewed by the HVID team privately. Outcomes are reflected as system-level signals — no public accusations are made.

No public shaming

HVID does not publish reports, expose reporters, or enable public defamation. The system exists for accountability, not exposure.

About HVID

Identity infrastructure

HVID was built on one principle: the internet needs a way to confirm that a digital account is controlled by a real, persistent human identity — without exposing personal data.

HVID is not a social network. It is not a rating system. It is not a scam detector. It is infrastructure — a neutral layer that confirms identity continuity and ownership.

Privacy-first by design. Minimal data. No public accusations. Accountability without exposure.
