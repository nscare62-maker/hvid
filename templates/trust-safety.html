To maintain absolute trust, HVID clearly defines the strict operational boundaries between verified identity signals and external behavioral risks.

Explicit Non-Claims

What HVID Does Not Guarantee

Subjective variables outside of cryptographic verification.

✕

Moral Safety & Good Intentions

Not Evaluated

Verification does not guarantee that a verified human will act safely or in good faith. Users must always exercise independent caution and common sense.

✕

Honesty, Credit or Fulfillment

Not Evaluated

HVID proves *who* you are transacting with, not whether they will tell the truth, fulfill contractual agreements, or pay their debts.

✕

Zero Risk in Private Deals

Not Evaluated

HVID is not an insurance policy, escrow service, or warranty. Having a verified identity does not eliminate economic or personal transaction risk.

✕

Platform Liability for Outcomes

Not Evaluated

HVID operates purely as an informational signal infrastructure and assumes zero liability for private interactions, contracts, or agreements between users.

Architectural Principle

Identity Certainty is Not a Reputation Score

Online review systems fail because subjective stars and ratings can be easily faked, brigaded, or extorted. HVID replaces subjective reviews with deterministic cryptographic certainty: we confirm the existence and ownership of the human actor, leaving behavioral discretion entirely in your hands.

How Verification Works
Integrity by Design

The Four Pillars of Systemic Trust

Engineered from the ground up to prevent identity abuse, data exploitation, and centralized overreach.

Deterministic Truth

Signals are derived from cryptographic matches, verifiable mobile ownership, and liveness challenges — eliminating guessing and rumor.

Zero Honey-Pot Privacy

We employ irreversible cryptographic hashes for sensitive attributes. HVID does not store unencrypted identification documents or sell user dossiers.

Anti-Sybil Resilience

A single human cannot spawn multiple active verified accounts. Coordinated troll farms and bot networks cannot obtain verified HVID status.

Protocol Neutrality

HVID functions strictly as an identity authentication layer. We do not police lawful expression, adjudicate opinions, or deplatform without evidentiary proof.

Enterprise Misconduct Handling

Private. Evidence-Based. Internally Adjudicated.

HVID maintains an audited private reporting system. We are not a public smear board or cancellation platform.

STEP 01

Confidential Submission

The identity of reporters is cryptographically masked and strictly sealed. Reported parties are never informed of who submitted the claim.

STEP 02

Rigorous Evidence Standard

All claims must include verifiable factual evidence (receipts, transaction proofs, or formal reports). Emotional accusations and hearsay are summarily discarded.

STEP 03

Internal Trust Audit

Evidence is evaluated under established evidentiary rubrics by specialized trust officers. Counterparties receive impartial opportunity to contest factual claims.

STEP 04

System Status Signal

Substantiated claims update machine-readable protocol states (Under Review, Suspended). We never publish public smear dossiers or engage in social shaming.

Strict Safeguards Against Weaponized Submissions: Malicious or fraudulent false reports violate the HVID Terms of Service and subject the reporting account to immediate permanent suspension.
